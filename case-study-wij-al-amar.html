How I Built Wij Al Amar: A Restaurant Website That Fills Tables

Jacob Qumsiyeh · ~3 min read

The Challenge

Wij Al Amar had no website at all, making a beloved Bethlehem dining spot nearly invisible to anyone searching online. New customers had no way to browse the menu, see the atmosphere, or find contact details before deciding to visit.

The Approach

I designed and built a full restaurant website with dedicated sections for the menu, gallery, location, and contact, optimised for mobile since most people looking for "where to eat nearby" are searching from their phone, and built with local search discoverability in mind from the start.

Design Thinking

The aesthetic is warm and inviting, matching the restaurant's own character. Strong imagery, clean typography, and clear calls to action build appetite and confidence in first-time visitors before they've even walked through the door.

The Result

A professional online presence that makes it easy for new customers to discover Wij Al Amar, see what they offer, and decide to visit, all before leaving the house.

Tech Stack

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript PHP
