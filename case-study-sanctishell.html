How I Built SanctiShell: A Fully-Owned, Fee-Free eCommerce Store

Jacob Qumsiyeh · ~4 min read

The Challenge

A Bethlehem-based artisan needed to sell handcrafted Mother-of-Pearl items online. The obvious route was Shopify or a similar hosted platform — fast to launch, but with ongoing monthly fees, transaction cuts, and real limits on customization. For a small, craft-focused business, those recurring costs eat directly into margins on every sale, and the storefront never truly belongs to the business, just to the platform.

The Approach

Instead of building on top of a third-party platform, I built SanctiShell as a fully custom eCommerce application from the ground up: product listings, a shopping cart, and a streamlined checkout, all owned outright by the client with no monthly subscription and no per-sale platform fee.

That decision changes the economics of the business permanently — every future sale keeps its full margin instead of feeding a platform's cut, and the client can extend or modify the store however the business grows, without waiting on a third-party's feature roadmap or pricing changes.

Design Thinking

The interface stays clean and product-focused, keeping the craftsmanship front and center rather than burying it under unnecessary UI. Navigation is simple by design, so customers move from discovery to purchase without friction — every screen exists to answer one question: does this help someone buy?

The Result

SanctiShell launched as a fully functional, fee-free storefront that gives the client complete control over products, pricing, and the customer experience, with zero platform dependency and zero recurring platform fees eating into their margins.
