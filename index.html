07 / FAQ

Common Questions

Everything clients usually ask before we start working together.

What's your process from start to finish?

We start with a free call to understand your goals, then I send a proposal covering scope, timeline, and price. Once approved, I build in stages and share progress along the way, so you're never waiting in the dark. After you approve the final result, I handle launch and a short handover.

How long does a typical project take?

A Starter site usually takes 1-2 weeks, and a Premium build takes 3-5 weeks depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back. I'll give you a firm timeline once we've scoped the project on our call.

What payment methods do you accept?

I accept bank transfer, Wise, and PayPal, with pricing shown in your local currency using the switcher on the Pricing section above. Most projects run on a 50% deposit to begin and 50% on delivery; larger projects can be split into milestones.

Do you offer support after the site launches?

Yes. Every project includes a short bug-fix window after launch, and I offer ongoing maintenance and update plans for clients who want their site actively looked after. Just ask on the call.

Do I need to already have a domain or hosting?

No. If you don't have a domain or hosting yet, I'll guide you through picking and setting them up as part of the project. If you already have them, I'll just need access to connect everything.

Can you work with clients outside your timezone?

Absolutely, I work with clients worldwide and stay flexible with async updates via email or WhatsApp, with calls scheduled at a time that works for both of us.
