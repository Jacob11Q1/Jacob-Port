How I Co-Built IQPREC: An Arabic-First FPL AI Platform

Jacob Qumsiyeh · ~4 min read

The Challenge

Fantasy Premier League has millions of players worldwide, including a large and growing Arab community, but every serious FPL tool is built in English, for a generic audience. Ask a general AI assistant like ChatGPT for advice and it doesn't know your real squad, doesn't have live FPL data, and doesn't understand what your mini-league rivals are playing. The recommendations are generic, and none of it speaks to Arab managers in their own language.

The Approach

I co-founded IQPREC with a friend in Beit Sahour, Palestine, and built it from the ground up: no investors, no outside funding, just the two of us writing the product by hand. Managers link their real FPL team ID, and IQPREC layers live FPL data, exclusive Arab-community ownership data, and Claude AI-powered analysis on top, delivered natively in Arabic rather than translated after the fact.

The backend runs on Node.js and Express with a self-hosted PostgreSQL database and Redis for performance, validating every recommendation against the official FPL API before it reaches a manager. The frontend is a React and Vite single-page app built for speed during the narrow window before every gameweek deadline.

Design Thinking

Every decision in FPL happens under time pressure, right before a deadline, so the interface is built for speed and clarity over decoration: a live gameweek ticker, at-a-glance captain and transfer recommendations, and a dashboard that surfaces the one decision that matters most instead of burying it in menus.

The Result

IQPREC is live today, self-funded with no outside investment, with a free tier and a paid Pro tier (€15/mo or €110/season) already in market. It's steadily growing within the Arab FPL community, serving AI-powered captain picks, transfer advice, and lineup recommendations every single gameweek.
